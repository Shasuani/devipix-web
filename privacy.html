Privacy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

DeviPix is a studio based in Greece. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to the information you send us. We keep it minimal on purpose.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email, and your message. We don't ask for anything else, and we don't run advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

To read your enquiry and reply to it. That's the only purpose.

Who processes it

  • Form delivery — your submission is handled by our form provider (Formspree) or sent directly to our email, then stored in our inbox so we can respond.
  • Hosting & anti-abuse — the site and the AI assistant run on Cloudflare, which may process technical data (like your IP address) to keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • AI assistant — if you chat with the on-site assistant, your messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a reply. Don't share sensitive personal data in that chat.

We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to help you, then delete them. Ask us any time and we'll remove your details sooner.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/UK you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you sent — just email us and we'll take care of it.

Questions or a deletion request? hello@devipix.com
